About me

Hello, I'm Breege! I teach English and Irish, and I have done it for more than 30 years. I am patient, I explain things clearly, and I want every student to leave a lesson feeling more confident.

  • I studied Irish at University College Galway and I am a fully qualified teacher (Higher Diploma in Education, TEFL).
  • I have worked at Tipperary FET College since 2002, and I was Acting Principal there in 2025–2026.
  • I know the Junior Cycle and Leaving Certificate Irish exams well.
  • I have taught in Ireland, the UK and Poland, in schools, colleges and adult education.
  • I have experience with learners of many backgrounds and with specific learning difficulties, such as dyslexia.
  • I live in Nenagh. When I am not teaching, you will find me walking, swimming or planning my next trip.

No. You only give your name and email when you book.

Any level. We find your level together in the free introduction.

Yes, in Nenagh. Book and pay online in the same way, then tell me that you would like to come in person.

Yes, with the link in your confirmation email. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the lesson.

Yes. I teach Irish to primary school children and to teenagers preparing for the Junior Cycle and Leaving Certificate. I teach English to children, teenagers and adults.
